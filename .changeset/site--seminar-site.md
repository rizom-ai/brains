A site for a brain that holds seminars: the home page opens on the brain's claim and lists its seminars, each seminar is one page at `/seminars/<id>`, the brain's notes have their place in the menu, and `/ask` offers the guest question box once asking is open. Drawn from the theme's tokens, for `@rizom/theme-books`.
