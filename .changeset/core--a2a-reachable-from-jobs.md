A background job can ask another brain. The worker process runs no interfaces, so a job that asked a peer through an A2A contract was told the brain could not reach other brains: a seminar's turns, and the daily refresh of what peers share with this brain. A first-party interface can now declare the contracts it answers for the brain's own packages (`withAnswers`), which the worker registers alone, as it registers an interface's channels; A2A answers its ask, turn and shared-entity contracts that way.
