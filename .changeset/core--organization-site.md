Team brains get their own site. The new organization site opens on an atlas of the agents the team has approved, placed around it the way the console's agent map places them and each linked to its agent page, under an opening taken from the anchor profile. New team brains scaffold it; existing brains keep the site they set in `brain.yaml`, and any team or organization brain can select `@brains/site-organization` there.
