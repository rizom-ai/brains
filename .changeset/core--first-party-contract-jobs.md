First-party entity packages can declare jobs other packages queue by contract, with the contract's input as the queued data, running as the package as their entity plugin classes' handlers did: with the image model, attachment resolution, the shared upload store, and links into a target that keep the generation-link rules and the target's visibility. Their create routes can write a pending placeholder and queue such a job, store a complete record under the next free id, or hand on the target they resolved. All of it is internal to the brain's own packages.
