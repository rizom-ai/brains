A site now accepts sections that bring their own drawing per language; until now `defineSite` refused them. The header and menu gloss each page in Chinese, and the language switch names the language being read first. Act IV's cards lie on their tilted grid again on every homepage, where the inner pages' stack had flattened them into a column.
