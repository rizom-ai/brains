Topic titles name one concept other brains can match. A title that joins two concepts ("Design and Craft", "Mind/Body"), or is built on a term the brain excludes through the topics plugin's new `excludeTitleTerms` (a book brain's author, so no "Nietzschean Philosophy"), never becomes a topic. An existing topic with such a title leaves once every source has been read, and the next-best challenger takes its place from the votes already stored.
