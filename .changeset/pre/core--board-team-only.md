The Board is now a team capability. `action-board` is no longer part of the `chat` bundle; the team recipe adds it alongside `docs`, so personal and professional brains no longer get the Board, Inbox card tracking or the `action_item_update` tool. Action items extracted from conversations still appear in the Library and on the Overview. A brain that wants the Board without the team posture adds it explicitly with `add: [action-board]`.
