The Fourth grows from one page into Sam's full site: What (See, Tell, Reach and their six services), Who (four worlds and their cases), How (one case in four moves, the pattern, Seamless Communication and ContentDNA™), About (the fourth wall, the mission, the cast, off stage) and Let's talk. The header names the pages and folds them into a menu on small screens, the footer lists them again, and the homepage's links lead to them. The pointer is the design's key. On phones the language switch moves into the menu, the arrow back up rests in the footer, and the service tiles, the ContentDNA match, the benefits, the portraits and the off-stage photos keep inside the screen.
