Contact and quiz are declarative services on the forms engine, keeping the plugin ids, dependencies, routes, pages, alert job, maintenance, Inbox sources, health and stored bytes their classes had. Contact requests and quiz results are a deliberate exception to metadata being the frontmatter, as mail items and traffic snapshots are: a read hands back the whole stored document, and metadata indexes only the title, times, status and alert state (and, for a quiz result, its quiz and whether the taker left an email), so names, email addresses, messages, answers and results never enter metadata.
