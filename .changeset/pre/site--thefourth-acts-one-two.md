The Fourth's homepage tells its first two acts. Act I, the hero, opens on red with the hero's work beside a paper arch, a balloon and an optional photo cut out as a keyhole (`![alt](entity://image/<id>)` in its content). Act II, the curse of jargon, says a true sentence nobody listened to: "Break the spell" scatters its words and strikes out the jargon, then a keyhole opens onto the translation in the spell's color, and "Try another spell" moves through the spells. Without JavaScript the sentence stands alone; with reduced motion the translation simply appears.
