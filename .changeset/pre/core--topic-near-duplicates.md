Near-duplicate topics merge again. Once every source has been read, topics whose stored embeddings sit close together are judged once by the merge prompt. A duplicate leaves, and its votes count for the topic it duplicates, so "Bildung", "Cultural Education" and "Educational Formation" no longer take three of a brain's topic slots. Distinct verdicts are remembered, and a brain without embeddings keeps every topic.
