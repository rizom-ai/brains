New site and theme for The Fourth, a brand consultancy in Taipei: the homepage opens on a paper stage between two curtains, with a red keyhole the visitor steps through as they scroll, under a header with the logo and the site's call to action and a footer with the sign-off and the email. The theme carries the paper, the ink, the stage lights and the type.
