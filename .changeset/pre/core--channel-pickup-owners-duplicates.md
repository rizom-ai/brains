The channel action item pickup leaves requests to the brain (an @mention and the brain's answer) to the brain itself, so asking the brain for a card no longer adds a second one. A task the messages state twice, or one the channel already has an open card for, is added once. A card goes to the member the messages name: a linked speaker is known by their member name, and a member who did not speak can own a card too.
