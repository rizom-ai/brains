Readers take a record's fields from its metadata, which keeps one shape whatever form its file is in, and its body from its content without any header; only a record with no metadata at all is read from its header. The style guide, ask-content, site info, publishing (covers, attachments, sources), social posts, posts, decks, FAQs and playbooks read this way, so a later change to how a type lays out its file does not reach them. A book reads its own documents, and the topics it shows, as stored.
