The hub's story tells why a hub is needed, in three chapters over the field of lights: the pipeline draining the region into the center, with a count of who left; the cohorts gathering around one connector and scattering when each program ends, with a button that lets the connector leave; and the hub that remembers, where each cohort leaves contours around the hub's Brain, with a switch to see the hub without it, a sparkline and a readout of what is on record. A rail of chapter numbers follows the reader, and on a phone each chapter's card docks below the drawing.
