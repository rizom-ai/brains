A brain that holds seminars shows its notes on its site. The note type has no pages of its own, so the seminar capability brings them: `/notes` lists every public note by title under its opening sentence, and `/notes/<id>` shows a note as written, its quotes and links kept. Brains without the seminar capability are unchanged.
