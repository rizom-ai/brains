An owner can show a peer under a name of their own. An agent in the directory takes an optional `shownAs`; where it is set, `network_ask` attributes the peer's answer and sources to that name, and a seminar is held and stored under it, so a brain that reads with Karl's brain can call him Marx in chat, in its seminars and on its pages alike. A card refresh and a reconnect keep the name, and a reconnect now keeps the owner's notes too. `plugins.seminar.peers` no longer carries names: it is the list of agent ids that orders the peers on the brain's pages.
