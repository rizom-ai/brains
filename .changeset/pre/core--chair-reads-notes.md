The chair of a seminar reads the brain's own notes on the question before it decides who stands. It is given the public notes nearest the question, at most three, as its own background: it judges the openings and writes its follow-ups with them, and puts none of their passages to a participant as his. A seminar is held without them when the notes cannot be read.
