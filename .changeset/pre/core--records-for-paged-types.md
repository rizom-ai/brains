Docs, guides, newsletter issues and books publish AT Protocol records, each under its own lexicon: `ai.rizom.brain.doc`, `ai.rizom.brain.guide`, `ai.rizom.brain.newsletter` and `ai.rizom.brain.book`. Docs, guides and sent newsletter issues carry their text; a book's record says what the book is and carries none of its text. The four lexicons enter the registry as drafts. A reference can point to any of the four.
