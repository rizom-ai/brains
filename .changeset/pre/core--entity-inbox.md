`defineEntity` accepts `inbox`, which registers an Inbox source for the type: items listed as the package, opened and acted on at the actor's visibility, and acted on through edits the brain refuses when the actor may not take the action or the record changed after it was read. `@rizom/brain/entities` exports the inbox declaration, its contexts and edits, `EntityRecordReader`, and `InboxItem`, `InboxAction`, `InboxActor`, `InboxItemDetail` and `InboxFacetDefinition`.
