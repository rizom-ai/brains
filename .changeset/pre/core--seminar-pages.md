A seminar has its pages. `seminar:seminar-list` lists the seminars a brain has held, newest first, each with its question, the day, who stood and who answered him, and the first sentence of where they meet. `seminar:seminar-detail` sets one seminar to be read top to bottom: the openings side by side, the chair's turn, the challenges, the rejoinder and the closing, with every source a turn returned linked where its text names it. `plugins.seminar.peers` names the brain's peers and orders them, as its pages show them and as the chair writes of them. `seminar:ask` is the Ask page of such a brain: the guest box while asking is open, and whom a question reaches.
