On a phone, Studio's sections are a tab bar along the bottom, replacing the Browse sheet.

- Home, Library, Publish, Chat and Brain each open their first page; the tab of the section you are in is marked, and a light shows when anything in it needs you.
- Tapping the tab you are in opens that section's other pages in a short sheet, with counts and what needs you.
- An open entry keeps its own bar and way back, so the tab bar steps aside while editing.
- The header keeps the brand mark where Browse was.
