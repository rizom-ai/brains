Studio's editor is built around writing:

- An open entry folds the navigation to its rail, and Properties sit beside the document as an inspector. Ctrl/⌘+\ puts them away for that type, and ? lists the editor's shortcuts.
- A document's title is its heading, edited in place. A new entry's file is named after its title, shown as "Saves as …" with a Change link.
- Entries open in Source, which reads like the document: the reading face, headings at their size, and only code in monospace. Phones open an entry on its writing.
- Unsaved edits are kept in this browser for a week, for each person and entry, and are offered back after a reload. Saving, Discard and signing out clear them, and fields that hold credentials are never kept.
- The footer is one line. Delete and sync details are in the ••• menu.
- A new entry says what it still needs: an empty required field is named as required, and the file name shows only its own issues.
- Ctrl/⌘+S saves wherever focus is while an entry is open.
