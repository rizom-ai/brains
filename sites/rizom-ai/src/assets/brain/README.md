# Brain landing-page captures

These are static captures, not live product widgets or evidence of an agent run.

- `chat-*`: Studio rebuilt at revision `2b7ecfe604`, with fictional, in-memory pilot notes and a seeded conversation. Nothing was generated by a running Brain, saved to app data or published during capture. Native desktop and mobile views are provided in Instrument and Paper climates.
- `network-*`: unmodified public Rizom dashboard captures from 8 September 2026. They show semantic proximity among saved agents, not collaboration or a live network feed.

The source PNG bytes are embedded losslessly in self-contained SVG files because the site package’s static-asset contract is text-based. No capture content was altered by this packaging. Each image remains independently addressable and opens at full resolution; the visible caption explains its status.

The current homepage’s datasource-backed maps are unchanged. Do not replace those maps with these snapshots, seed their contents into the app, or describe these captures as live.

Original sources and capture provenance are retained in the local design study under `docs/design/rizom-brain-page` in the main development checkout.
