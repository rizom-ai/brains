Sessions are the workspace's spine, not a casualty of the move. The familiar session rail — titles, rename, delete, new conversation — becomes the workspace's left column on desktop and stays the drawer on the phone. URL identity and restoration change underneath it; the interaction model does not.

"Discuss in chat" creates a titled conversation. The handoff starts or resumes the actor's session for that item, so the discussion is findable later in the list — context is not a mode, it is a conversation like any other.

The strip slims to Dashboard / Studio. Chat is trusted-only, so its separate strip entry retires. Dashboard gains no public Chat door in this integration; anonymous or sign-in-only Chat discovery remains a separate product decision.

Chrome budget: two bars and two lines. Strip collapses into the top bar, the chips row scrolls with a visible fade, and the head is one line — title, tab, attention chip. Content starts at roughly 160px instead of 450px.
