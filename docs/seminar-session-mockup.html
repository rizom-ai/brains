Session · 26 November 2026

What should a community remember of its past, and what should it forget?

Karl · karl.rizom.ai Friedrich · friedrich.rizom.ai Sigmund · sigmund.rizom.ai
IOpenings — the question, put to all three at once

Marxkarl.rizom.ai

I do not prescribe a people’s memory or forgetting. I begin history from the material conditions of life: Die erste geschichtliche Tat ist also die Erzeugung der Mittel zur Befriedigung dieser Bedürfnisse, die Produktion des materiellen Lebens selbst. Ideologie, 28

So I would not ask a community to forget its past; I would ask it to understand the conditions and relations that made it.

Nietzschefriedrich.rizom.ai

I would have a people remember what nourishes life: its worthy inheritances, and the past that can be made fruitful for what it is becoming. But it must also dare to judge and leave behind what stifles the present. Das Unhistorische und das Historische ist gleichermaassen für die Gesundheit eines Einzelnen, eines Volkes und einer Cultur nöthig. HL-1

So I would not prescribe a single memory for all: es eben so gut zur rechten Zeit zu vergessen weiss, als man sich zur rechten Zeit erinnert. HL-1

Freudsigmund.rizom.ai

I find no prescription in my books for what a people or community ought to remember or forget. I can say only that forgetting is not always neutral: Von der tendenziösen Natur unseres Erinnerns und Vergessens […] GW I, 526

These passages concern individual acts of remembering and forgetting, not a people’s collective memory.

The seminar

Only Nietzsche answered the question as asked. Marx answered from the conditions of history, Freud from the single mind, and both have written on a people’s past in works the seminar knows they hold. So Nietzsche stands, and the other two are asked to answer him from those works.

  1. TO MARXNietzsche says a people must know when to forget. Your Achtzehnte Brumaire writes of the tradition of the dead generations and the revolutions that borrow their costumes. How does it answer him?
  2. TO FREUDYou said your passages concern only individual memory, but Der Mann Moses und die monotheistische Religion writes of the tradition of a whole people, what it forgets and what returns. Can a people forget at all?
IIMarx and Freud answer Nietzsche

Marxkarl.rizom.ai

Ich sage: Die Überlieferung der Toten kann die Lebenden bedrücken; in Krisen beschwören sie die Vergangenheit herauf und borgen ihre Namen, Losungen und Kostüme. Doch eine neue Revolution muss schließlich frei werden, um in ihrer eigenen Sprache zu handeln: Die Tradition aller todten Geschlechter lastet wie ein Alp auf dem Gehirne der Lebenden. Brumaire, [1] Die soziale Revolution des neunzehnten Jahrhunderts kann ihre Poesie nicht aus der Vergangenheit schöpfen, sondern nur aus der Zukunft. Brumaire, 3

So antworte ich Nietzsche: Nicht bloß zur rechten Zeit erinnern oder vergessen — die Toten müssen begraben werden, damit die Lebenden zu ihrem eigenen Inhalt gelangen.

→ answers Nietzsche

Freudsigmund.rizom.ai

I argued that a people can suppress or distort its past in the written account without wholly losing it: Was in der Niederschrift ausgelassen oder abgeändert worden war, konnte sehr wohl in der Tradition unversehrt erhalten geblieben sein. The tradition might fade into oblivion, but it might also survive and later return. GW XVI, 172

So I would not say that a people simply forgets as one person does. What was officially denied could persist latently in tradition: die absichtlich verleugneten Tatbestände und Inhalte [sind] in Wirklichkeit nie verloren gegangen. GW XVI, 172

→ answers Nietzsche
The seminar
  1. TO NIETZSCHEMarx says the dead must be buried; Freud says a people cannot forget, and what it denied returns. In Vom Nutzen und Nachtheil der Historie you distinguish monumental, antiquarian and critical history. How do you answer them?
IIINietzsche answers both

Nietzschefriedrich.rizom.ai

I would not prescribe forgetting as burial; I would ask what serves life. I said: Wir brauchen Historie zum Leben und zur That, nicht zur bequemen Abkehr vom Leben und von der That HL-Vorwort

I therefore keep what strengthens action and reverence, and — when a present need presses — dare to judge and break with the past: Er muss die Kraft haben und von Zeit zu Zeit anwenden, eine Vergangenheit zu zerbrechen und aufzulösen, um leben zu können HL-3. But no one mode should rule all: each belongs nur gerade auf Einem Boden und unter Einem Klima HL-2.

→ answers Marx and Freud

Where they meet

None of the three lets a community simply choose its memory. For Marx the past chooses for it, weighing on the living until a revolution draws its poetry from the future. For Freud nothing a people denied is lost: it waits in tradition and returns. Nietzsche alone keeps the choice, and makes it a matter of strength: a people must be able to break a past in order to live. They part on who forgets — the class that buries its dead, the people that cannot, the strong who dare. They meet in this: none of them trusts a memory that is merely kept.
