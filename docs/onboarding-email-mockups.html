Onboarding emails

Draft copy for plans/onboarding-emails.md. Sample values: expiry Tuesday 6 October 2026 at 14:00 UTC.

Anchor setup email

Sent once when a new brain boots without a passkey. Sample brain: yeehaa.rizom.ai.

HTML part

Your brain is ready — set up your passkey To: you@example.com

yeehaa.rizom.ai

Your brain is ready

Your brain at yeehaa.rizom.ai is set up and waiting for you. Set up your passkey to sign in.

Set up your passkey

A passkey replaces a password: your device confirms it’s you with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. It takes under a minute.

This link works once and expires on Tuesday 6 October 2026 at 14:00 UTC. Don’t forward it.

What happens next

  1. Register your passkey. You’re signed in and taken to your dashboard.
  2. Open chat and say hello. Tell it what you’re working on — your brain holds your notes, links and ideas, and gets more useful the more you add.
  3. Use Studio to see and edit everything your brain holds.

Button not working? Paste this link into your browser:
https://yeehaa.rizom.ai/setup?token=setup_…

Connect other AI tools to your brain at https://yeehaa.rizom.ai/mcp.

Didn’t expect this email? Ignore it — the link expires on its own.

Invitation

Sent when an Admin invites someone. Sample: Sam Jansen invites a trusted member to the Rizom brain at team.rizom.ai. Without a known inviter the subject reads “You’re invited to the Rizom brain” and the first sentence drops “Sam Jansen invited you”.

HTML part

Sam Jansen invited you to the Rizom brain To: alex@example.com

team.rizom.ai

You’re invited to the Rizom brain

Sam Jansen invited you to join the Rizom brain at team.rizom.ai as a trusted member. Set up your passkey to accept.

Accept and set up your passkey

A passkey replaces a password: your device confirms it’s you with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. It takes under a minute.

This link works once and expires on Tuesday 6 October 2026 at 14:00 UTC. Don’t forward it — it’s tied to you.

What happens next

  1. Register your passkey. You’re signed in straight away.
  2. Open chat and ask the brain what it knows — it answers from everything shared in it.
  3. Use Studio to browse what the brain holds.

Button not working? Paste this link into your browser:
https://team.rizom.ai/setup?token=setup_…

Didn’t expect this email? Ignore it — the link expires on its own.
