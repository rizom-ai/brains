What changes

  • The title stays “Overview”; one quiet status line under it says whether the brain is healthy. The count of what needs you sits on the Needs you heading.
  • Last 24 hours shows every event as a light on a time band. Selecting one highlights its row below.
  • Needs you is ranked: failures first, then reviews, then queues. Each item carries its own actions.
  • What happened leaves out anything already in Needs you, groups by time of day, and names things in plain words.
  • Sources show their figures while collapsed; expanding reveals the source’s own view.
  • Brain facts are always visible instead of hidden in two disclosures.

What it needs from the runtime

  • Status line: a facts list on the view, each a label and tone, composed from readiness.
  • Band: a new timeline panel block; the activity registry keeps 24 hours instead of the last 12 events.
  • Needs you: attention list items gain severity rank and inline actions (list items already carry actions).
  • Sources: disclosure cards render their digest lines in the summary row.
  • Brain: the System and Network cards merge into one plain key-values card.
