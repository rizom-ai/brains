Today's preview on the left and the proposal on the right, one row per state. The two answer phones on the right scroll. What the rows share: the name appears once per screen, the map stays in one place from the moment the sheet opens, text never slides under a hard edge, and the sources list uses the map's own shapes. The map drawing is schematic; the live map keeps its contours and labels.

Homepage

Homepage ask

  • The box says what it is for. Today the field is empty; it gets its placeholder, "Ask about my work…".

The sheet opens

  • The starting questions fill the space the map left, so the sheet opens with something to tap.
  • One rule above the composer. Today there are two hairlines stacked eight pixels apart.
  • One note, the same in every state. Today it changes from "Public knowledge…" to "Answers come from what Yeehaa has published." after the first question.

Waiting

  • The waiting line names the work, not the person: "Looking through the essays and talks".
