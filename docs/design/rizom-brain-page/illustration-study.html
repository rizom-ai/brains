Rizom Brain · Illustration study

A brain of your own.

Your knowledge gives it context. Capabilities give it something to do. Connections let it reach further.

One agent. Shaped around your work.

Bring the material, choose what it can do, and decide how it connects. Your brain remains your system—not part of somebody else’s shared brain.

The buttons explain relationships; they don’t configure a brain or make requests. External AI providers can receive request context. No automatic peer selection, private access, or completed work is implied.
