    Rizom Hub · Eindhoven

    A place that keeps what its ecosystem learns.

    Organizations, researchers and independent people already meet here to work on questions that matter to them. The hub makes sure what they learn stays: with them, with the hub, and with whoever comes next.

    Scroll to see why
    01 · The pipeline

    Innovation runs one way.

    The way we organize innovation was built for the center. A region finds, trains and funds young companies, and the good outcomes are two: they become a platform, or they are bought by one.

    The best companies move or are sold, the founders follow the capital, and what the ecosystem learned along the way leaves with them.

    02 · Programs end

    Every program starts from zero.

    A hub lives on programs and calls. Funding comes in cycles, people move on between them, and what holds it all together is usually one person: the connector who knows everyone.

    01cohort · carried over from the last one: nothing
    03 · A hub that remembers

    Each cohort starts where the last stopped.

    The hub keeps its own memory: who is there, what they know, which introductions worked, what each program learned.

    It lives in the hub's Brain, in plain files the hub owns, with an agent that works for the hub and nobody else.

    0people and introductions on record when cohort 01 starts
    04 · Labs

    A lab, not a desk.

    Organizations come to the hub to work on one question of their own, away from the daily work and next to others doing the same.

    1. Weeks 1–3
      The audit      Interviews and a workshop: who knows what, where knowledge leaks, and what an agent must never do.
    2. The pilotEvery decision goes into the lab's Brain with its reasons and sources. The hub brings in the people the question needs.
    3. The Brain in useColleagues outside the lab ask what the team knows, and get answers with the sources behind them.
    4. The endThe organization takes its Brain home: hosted, on its own servers, or as plain files.

    05 · How it fits into Rizom

    Each holds what it should.

    There is no platform in the middle. The terms are written down, and the same in every hub.

    • OrganizationsTheir Brains and everything in them. They take them anywhere; no one can sell them.
    • The hubThe space, the community, the focus, and its own Brain, held for its community.
    • MembersA practice: they certify, deliver labs and build on their own Brain.
    • Rizom B.V.Sets up the Brains, hosts them, and delivers labs until the hub's own people are certified.
    • Rizom FoundationHolds the method, the platform and the mark as a commons that cannot be sold.
    60% delivery · Rizom B.V.15% hub25% commons
    06 · The network

    Many hubs, no center.

    The hub's Brain connects to other hubs, at home and abroad. Organizations and members find each other's expertise across them, within what each allows.

    Growth comes from connection rather than concentration. Every hub stays itself, and none of them is the center.
