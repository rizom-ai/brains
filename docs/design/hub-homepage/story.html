    Rizom Hub

    Programs come and go. The hub stays.

    Every ecosystem has places where companies, researchers, public bodies and capital meet. Programs pass through them; the place remains. This hub is built to keep what passes through it.

    Scroll to see why
    01 · The intake

    The hub trains. The center harvests.

    Most hubs work as the intake of a pipeline built for the center. A region finds its most promising companies, trains them, funds them, and sends them on.

    The best ones move or are sold, the founders follow the capital, and what the hub learned along the way leaves with them.

    02 · Programs end

    Every call starts from zero.

    A hub lives on programs and calls. Funding comes in cycles, people move on between them, and what holds it all together is usually one person: the connector who knows everyone.

    01cohort of 03 · carried over from the last one: nothing
    03 · A hub that remembers

    Each cohort starts where the last stopped.

    The hub keeps its own memory: who is there, what they know, which introductions worked, what each program learned.

    It lives in the hub's Brain, in plain files the hub owns, with an agent that works for the hub and nobody else.

    0people and introductions on record when cohort 01 starts
    04 · What passes through

    Organizations come with a question. They leave with a Brain.

    A lab is six months, a team of four and one question of the organization's own, worked on at the hub, next to other labs and the people the hub brings in. Members who work for themselves certify here and deliver the labs.

    1. Weeks 1–3
      The audit      Interviews and a workshop: who knows what, where knowledge leaks, and what an agent must never do.
    2. The pilotEvery decision goes into the lab's Brain with its reasons and sources. The hub's members work alongside the team.
    3. The Brain in useColleagues outside the lab ask what the team knows, and get answers with the sources behind them.
    4. The endThe organization takes its Brain home. The hub keeps what the lab taught about the work, never its content.
    The Rizom Lab · six months · a team of four · €49,600 excl. VAT, the same in every hub
    05 · What the hub writes down

    Institutions stand on social contracts.

    At a hub, organizations first learn to rely on each other. So the hub writes down what is held in common, what cannot be sold and how the returns flow back, the same in every hub.

    Every lab: 60% to whoever delivers · 15% to the hub · 25% back to the commons
    06 · What the hub connects to

    Many hubs, no center.

    The hub's Brain connects to other hubs, at home and abroad. Organizations and members find each other's expertise across them, within what each allows.

    Seen from here, this hub sits in the middle. Seen from any other, so does that one.
