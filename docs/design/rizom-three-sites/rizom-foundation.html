    Rizom Foundation

    The case for collective intelligence.

    A world in which intelligence is collective. People and organizations hold what they know, and why, so nothing depends on one indispensable person or one platform.

    Memory without a home settles wherever it is cheapest to keep: with a few indispensable people, or in a model owned by one company. The Foundation builds the commons that gives it a home instead, and tests every assumption behind it in public.

    Founding supporters

    Start the first year, with no say attached.

    The Foundation takes no investors, so that its work stays open for good and belongs to no one. Its first year is started by founding supporters instead. We ask €50,000 in founding support, and any amount is welcome, from individuals and from organizations.

    As a gift once, or yearly for five years
    A person or an organization gives once or commits to a fixed amount each year for five years. For Dutch taxpayers the five-year form is fully deductible; the Foundation is applying for recognition as a public-benefit institution (ANBI).
    As a sponsor for organizations
    Everything founding supporters get, plus the sponsor's name on this site, in the code repository and at public programs, and seats at the roundtables. No say over the research, the code or the name.

    A pledge buys no stake and no vote. Founding supporters get a quarterly founders' letter, a pledge they can trace to the work it paid for, their name among the founders if they want it, and a place in the network.

    Why a foundation

    Infrastructure people depend on should not belong to whoever happens to sell it.

    The Foundation owns the code, the method and the Rizom name. Rizom B.V. runs hosting and client work under license, cannot take the commons with it, and pays 25% of every engagement back. An independent board majority decides on the license, the name and the company, and the Foundation takes no investors.
