AI that stays yours

I work on how organizations hold what they know, and what happens to that now that AI can read all of it.

Most of what's on offer moves your knowledge onto a platform and rents you back a smarter version. I think it should stay where it was made, and the tools should come to it. That's what I'm building with Rizom.

We're a research network with fifteen years of reports and mailing lists. What would “stays ours” actually look like for us?
Yeehaa's brainfrom public notes

For a network like yours it means three things. The reports and list archives are imported into a knowledge base you run, on your own domain, with the originals kept as files you can walk away with. Agents that answer questions or draft summaries read from that base and nothing else. And membership is yours to define: who can ask, who can add, who can see what.

Jan Hein wrote about the first two in the essay below. I don't have public notes on pricing or a rollout for a network your size. If you want that, ask him directly.
