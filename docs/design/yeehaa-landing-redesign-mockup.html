We’re a research network with fifteen years of reports and mailing lists. Where would we even start?

Jan Hein’s brain from his published work

He’d start with people, not the reports. In Hiding in Plain Sight he argues institutions rarely lack information; they have a surplus of traces that no longer add up to memory.1 Fifteen years of reports is likely that surplus.

The first step is mapping who in the network knows what, and whose judgment settles which questions, before any software is involved.2 Point an AI tool at the archive before that and it multiplies whatever confusion is already there.3

There’s nothing public here on pricing or on networks your size. That one is for him.
