Operator console · dataspace interaction proposal

A dataspace is a place, an offer is just mail.

How TrustFlow and a brain compose, at two levels. The inbox is where a peer's offer competes for attention alongside everything else that arrived — no new surface, no new authorization. The dataspace workspace is where the relationship lives: peers, catalogue, the agreements we hold, and what we publish back. Events are the first asset type through it, not the shape of it.

01 · Levels

Inbox decides, workspace holds

The inbox is for the moment of judgement and is deliberately generic. The workspace is for the standing relationship — peers, terms, provenance. Splitting them keeps attention short-lived and the relationship durable.

02 · Naming

Dataspace, not events

An events workspace would bake the demo vertical into the UI and force a second workspace the moment we share a link. Asset types are tabs inside one dataspace surface; events is simply the first with a registered projection.

03 · Trust

Triage is not authorization

Auto / Review / Ignore governs queueing only. Inbound trust stays with the agent directory's one approval per peer. The two are never merged, so a triage preference can never mint access.

04 · Provenance

Claim exactly what they claim

We store the agreement id and canonical hash, show both copies side by side, and repeat their own caveat: auditable, not independently verified. No re-derivation, no stronger claim than the dataspace makes.
