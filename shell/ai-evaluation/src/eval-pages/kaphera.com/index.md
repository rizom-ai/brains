# Kaphera

Kaphera builds infrastructure that lets enterprise AI systems find and query supply chain data held by partner organizations, while each organization keeps governance and compliance under its own control.

## Data with a contract attached

Kaphera runs managed data space connectors built on Eclipse Dataspace Components. Every exchange carries a machine-enforced contract that says who may access the data, for what purpose and for how long.

## In production

Its connectors already run in data spaces for the mobility and tire industries, with hundreds of active connectors.

## Why it matters

Autonomous data discovery lowers the cost of finding partner data, the contracts help meet emerging rules such as digital product passports, and organizations can grow from a single data-sharing agreement to ecosystem-wide collaboration.

## How it is owned

Kaphera is structured as steward-owned infrastructure rather than proprietary technology, and contributes to open source.
