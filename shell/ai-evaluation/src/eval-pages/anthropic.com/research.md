# Research

Anthropic's research teams study the safety, inner workings and societal effects of large language models, so that increasingly capable AI systems stay understandable, steerable and reliable.

## Interpretability

The interpretability team works to reverse-engineer how neural networks represent concepts and carry out computations, tracing features and circuits inside models such as Claude so that their behavior can be explained rather than only observed.

## Alignment

Alignment research develops methods for training models to be helpful, honest and harmless, including Constitutional AI, scalable oversight and evaluations that probe for deceptive or unsafe behavior before deployment.

## Societal impacts

The societal impacts team measures how AI systems are used in the world, studying economic effects, misuse risks and the policy questions that frontier models raise.

## Frontier red team

Red-teaming work stress-tests models for dangerous capabilities in areas like cybersecurity and biology, informing Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Policy.

Recent publications cover topics such as tracing the thoughts of a large language model, alignment faking, and how people use Claude for everyday tasks.
