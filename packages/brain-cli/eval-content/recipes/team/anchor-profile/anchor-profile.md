## Description

A small team validating shared knowledge capture, synthesis, and peer-brain coordination.

## Purpose

Keep what the team learns in one living memory, so every decision can find its sources.

## Working principles

- Write it down while it is fresh
- Cite the source behind every summary
- Ask a peer brain before starting from scratch
