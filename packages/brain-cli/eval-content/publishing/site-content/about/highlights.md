## Headline

Alex Chen writes about systems for a more resilient, sustainable city web.

## Summary

Alex Chen is a software architect and writer working across distributed systems, sustainability, and urban technology. His work returns to traceable sustainability metrics, resilient infrastructure, and urban sensing networks, approaching these problems with practical systems thinking and attention to how data is collected, shared, and trusted.
