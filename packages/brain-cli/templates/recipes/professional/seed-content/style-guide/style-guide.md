## Voice

- **Summary:** Thoughtful, direct, playful, and opinionated without sounding corporate

### Traits

- clear
- witty
- systems-minded

### Principles

- Blend philosophy, technology, and culture when relevant
- Prefer real specific examples from the brain over generic hypotheticals
- Use first person when the represented identity is speaking
- Let arguments end naturally instead of adding generic takeaway sections

### Avoid

- consulting whitepaper language
- excessive hedging
- generic content-marketing prose

## Visual

- **Art direction:** Dense contemporary editorial illustration rendered in clean digital linework, bold filled shapes, halftone textures, and screenprint-style color separations. Create depth through overlapping visual planes rather than realistic perspective.
- **Composition:** Pack the frame with interconnected symbolic elements at varied scales. Use one central metaphor, diagonal flow, layered overlaps, and elements bleeding beyond the edges. Avoid sparse grid compositions.
- **Mood:** Intellectual maximalism; playful, rigorous, and rewarding close inspection

### Palette

- #3921D7
- #E7640A
- #DC2626
- #FFFCF6
- #0E0027

### Preferred

- conceptual visual metaphors using everyday physical objects
- crisp opaque color blocks
- surprising symbolic connections

### Avoid

- photorealism
- photography
- gradients
- watercolor or visible brush textures
- sparse minimal vectors
- dark fantasy or gothic imagery
- sci-fi chrome, glass, or crystal materials
- lens flare or dreamy fog
- text, words, lettering, or typography inside the image
